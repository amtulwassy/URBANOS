    🛡️ Flood Safety Tips

    • Move valuables and electrical appliances to higher ground before water rises.
    • Avoid walking or driving through moving flood water — 15cm can knock you off your feet.
    • Turn off electricity and gas at the mains if instructed by authorities.
    • Keep an emergency kit ready: torch, radio, medicines, drinking water, dry food.
    • Never enter underpasses or basements during heavy rainfall.
    • Follow official URBANOS alerts, not rumours — use the Report Flood button to help others.
